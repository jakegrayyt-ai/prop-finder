How to read the board

Green hit rates mean the player has reached the line more often than Kalshi’s price implies. Red means less often.

Model is a stats-only estimate from this season and last season, adjusted for the matchup, home or away, the game’s betting total and rest. Edge is the expected profit per $1 contract after Kalshi’s fee. It leans on Kalshi’s price, and leans harder when the two disagree a lot, because a big gap usually means news the stats don’t have yet.
